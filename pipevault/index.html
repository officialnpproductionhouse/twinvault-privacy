Pipe Vault – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

Pipe Vault is a puzzle game made by NP Production House. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Contact: officialnpproductionhouse@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars and settings (music, sound effects, vibration) on your device only. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Ads

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the Stats screen, full-screen ads when you complete or open a level, and an ad you can watch to get a hint. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate a general location), device and app details, diagnostic information and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where a consent choice is offered in the game, you can answer it there. Uninstalling the game removes the progress saved on your device. Because the game has no accounts, we hold no personal data about you that we could delete.

Children

Pipe Vault is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this policy and the date above.

Contact
