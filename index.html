Privacy Policy for Twin Vault

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Twin Vault ("the app") is a puzzle game by NP Production House ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses. Contact: officialnpproductionhouse@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, level records, achievements and settings (sound, music, vibration) are stored only on your device. We do not run any account system and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a short ad between levels and an optional rewarded video you can watch to get a hint. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change this choice later from the app's settings screen when it is offered, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google (AdMob), as described above. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

Your choices

You can stop all data use by the app by uninstalling it. Uninstalling removes the progress stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.

Contact
